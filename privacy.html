Who we are

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, sign-up forms or online payments. When you add items to your order list or pick a date, that is saved only in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. It is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Orders on WhatsApp

When you tap an order button, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send. The details you share with us there, such as your name, phone number, delivery address and order details, are used only to make and deliver your order. We never sell or share them for marketing.

Payments

Payments are made by UPI directly from your own banking app. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Services from other companies

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg, and it links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. When you use these, their own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your order details or chat history at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Changes
