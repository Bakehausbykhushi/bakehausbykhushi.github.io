How ordering works

  1. 1

    Pick your bakes

    On the menu, tap the WhatsApp button on any item, or add a few and send them together.

  2. 2

    Tell us the details

    Date, weight, design, message on the cake. The message is pre-filled, so just fill the gaps.

  3. 3

    Confirm and pay

    We confirm availability and the final price. Pay the advance by UPI to lock your date.

  4. 4

    Fresh on the day

    Baked fresh for your date, ready for pickup or delivery as agreed on chat.
